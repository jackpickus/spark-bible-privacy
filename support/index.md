## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the app, go to **Settings → Manage Subscription**, and cancel from there — this
opens Apple's or Google's own subscription management. You can also cancel directly in
your device's **App Store** or **Google Play** account settings. Canceling stops future
renewals; you'll keep access through the end of the period you already paid for.

### Do I need an account to use the app?

No. When you request your first passage you can choose **Continue as guest**, and you can
keep using the app — and subscribe — that way for as long as you like.

The one thing an account gives you is a **saved passage history**. As a guest your
passages aren't saved, so there's nothing to sync between devices and nothing to recover
if you reinstall. You can sign in later at any time from **Settings → Sign In**, and your
subscription comes with you.

### How do I restore a purchase?

Open **Settings → Restore Purchases**. This works whether or not you're signed in, so
it's also how you recover a subscription bought as a guest after reinstalling or moving
to a new phone. Purchases are tied to the Apple ID or Google account you bought them
with, so use the same one.

### How many passages do I get, and when do they reset?

Daily passages are a subscription feature. Subscribing to Basic or Premium starts a
**3-day free trial** — no charge during the trial, and you can cancel any time before it
ends. **The trial is not throttled:** you get your full allowance from the first day,
which is 10 passages/day on Basic and 25/day on Premium. Your remaining passages for
today and, if applicable, your trial end date are shown in **Settings**. The count resets
at midnight UTC, which may be a few hours off from your local midnight.

### How do I delete my account?

Go to **Settings → Delete Account**. This permanently deletes your account and reading
history and cannot be undone. Deleting your account does **not** cancel an active
subscription — cancel that separately in your App Store or Google Play settings first
if you don't want to be charged again.

If you're using the app as a guest you won't see this option, because there's no account
and no saved history to delete. Email us if you'd like your guest identifier removed —
cancel any active subscription with Apple or Google first, as deleting it also removes
the record that connects you to that subscription.

### How do I turn off daily reminders?

Go to **Settings → Daily reminder** and toggle it off, or disable notifications for
MyBibleSpark in your device's system settings.

### The app says I've hit my daily limit

Your daily passage limit depends on your subscription tier — see **Settings** for your
current limit and how many you've used today. It resets at midnight UTC, which may be
a few hours off from your local midnight.

Guests and signed-in users get exactly the same allowance; continuing as a guest doesn't
reduce what you can read.

### It's asking me to subscribe, but I already have

Tap **Already subscribed? Restore** on that screen, or go to **Settings → Restore
Purchases**. This usually means your subscription is attached to a session this device no
longer has — most often after a device restore or a switch to a new phone while using the
app as a guest. Restoring reattaches it to this device. Use the same Apple ID or Google
account you originally subscribed with.

### I'm having trouble signing in

Make sure you're connected to the internet and using the same sign-in method (Apple or
Google) you used originally — each is treated as a separate account. Remember you don't
have to sign in at all: **Continue as guest** works for everything except saved history.
If you're still stuck, email us and we'll help sort it out.
