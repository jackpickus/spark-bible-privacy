## Frequently Asked Questions

### How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the app, go to **Settings → Manage Subscription**, and cancel from there — this
opens Apple's or Google's own subscription management. You can also cancel directly in
your device's **App Store** or **Google Play** account settings. Canceling stops future
renewals; you'll keep access through the end of the period you already paid for.

### How do I restore a purchase?

If you've reinstalled the app or signed in on a new device with the same account,
open **Settings → Manage Subscription**, which includes a Restore Purchases option.
Subscriptions are tied to the account you originally subscribed with — signing in with
a different provider (Apple vs. Google) counts as a different account and won't
automatically carry over an existing subscription.

### How many free passages do I get, and when do they reset?

New accounts can start a 7-day free trial (1 passage/day) when subscribing to Basic —
no charge during the trial, and you can cancel any time before it ends. Basic and
Premium subscribers get 10 and 25 passages/day. Your daily count and, if applicable,
your trial end date are shown in **Settings**, and resets at midnight UTC.

### How do I delete my account?

Go to **Settings → Delete Account**. This permanently deletes your account and reading
history and cannot be undone. Deleting your account does **not** cancel an active
subscription — cancel that separately in your App Store or Google Play settings first
if you don't want to be charged again.

### How do I turn off daily reminders?

Go to **Settings → Daily reminder** and toggle it off, or disable notifications for
MyBibleSpark in your device's system settings.

### The app says I've hit my daily limit

Your daily passage limit depends on your subscription tier — see **Settings** for your
current limit and how many you've used today. It resets at midnight UTC, which may be
a few hours off from your local midnight.

### I'm having trouble signing in

Make sure you're connected to the internet and using the same sign-in method (Apple or
Google) you used originally — each is treated as a separate account. If you're still
stuck, email us and we'll help sort it out.
